Worktop
Screw up through the front rail into 
your worktop to secure it in place. 
Screws for attaching to worktop are 
not supplied as these vary depending 
on worktop material and thickness. Ensure 
appropriate fixings for attaching worktop are 
used. Please refer to the specialist worktop 
supplier if these are required for solid surface 
worktops.
Securing to Adjacent Units
Screw into any side units using 
the 30mm screws (K) provided to 
secure to unit. Screw at the top and 
bottom of both sides of the unit, place a 
cover cap (M) on the head to conceal it.
L Brackets
Use 2 x 15mm screws (L) to secure each 
of the L brackets to the cabinets at either side. 
Then screw up through into the worktop as shown. 
Screws for attaching to worktop are 
not supplied as these vary depending 
on worktop material and thickness. Ensure 
appropriate fixings for attaching worktop are 
used. L brackets should only be fitted for securing 
wood and laminate worktops, please refer to the 
specialist worktop supplier if these are required 
for solid surface worktops.
