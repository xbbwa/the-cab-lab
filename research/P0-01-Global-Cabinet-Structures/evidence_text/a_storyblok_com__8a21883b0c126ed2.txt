Calculate the desired height of your basket and runners.  
Mark out and pre-drill two holes on each side panel of the cabinet 
as pictured above. Use a 2mm drill bit to pre-drill holes to a depth 
of roughly 2.5mm. Note: Ensure the operation of the pullout 
basket will not be affected by any obstructions in the cabinet like 
hinges prior to pre-drilling holes.
Align the runners (A) with the pre-drilled holes and using  
4 x wood screws (B), fix the basket runners to the cabinet sides. 
Use a further 8 x 16mm wood screws to secure runners, ensuring 
the screws are spaced evenly on both runners and are mirrored 
from the left to right side.
Place wire basket onto the runners, ensuring the metal supports 
are placed between the 2nd and 3rd wires at the front and back 
of the basket. Align the basket clips (C) and fix into place using 
the basket clip screws (D) and a hand held screw driver. 
If you are installing multiple baskets in the same cabinet or pantry, 
repeat steps 1-3 ensuring you leave enough clearance between  
the baskets to accommodate the desired items. Note: If you intend to 
use the door mount hardware to attach a cabinet door or pot drawer 
panel, take into consideration their size, weight and positioning as 
well (see steps 5-11). The maximum weight capacity for the basket 
includes the weight of the cabinet door or pot drawer panel  
if installed.

Caring for your kaboodle cabinets
Looking after your cabinets is really easy and with the right care and maintenance 
they will last you for many years. Just follow these simple steps and if you need any 
more information you can contact us: Australia: 1800 666 078 Monday to Friday 
between 8:00am and 6:00pm AEST. New Zealand: 0800 522 663 Monday to Friday 
between 8:00am and 5:00pm NZST or email: info@kaboodlegroup.com.au.
Caring for your kaboodle parts and accessories: Looking after your parts and 
accessories is really easy and with the right care and maintenance they will last  
you for many years. Just follow these simple steps. Hinges, runners and other parts 
and accessories should be kept dry at all times. To keep the mechanisms performing  
at their best, regularly wipe them with a dry cloth removing any dust and grime  
that may build up. In case of spills, clean immediately with a dry cloth. Do not use 
harsh abrasives.
